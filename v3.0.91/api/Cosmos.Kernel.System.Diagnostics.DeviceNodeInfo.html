Point-in-time snapshot of one device node in the driver kit's tree, produced by TryGetNode(int, out DeviceNodeInfo). The snapshot is taken without locking the kit, so a node whose offer or teardown is running on the kit worker may read one job stale: its state and the counts that depend on its binding can lag the log by one line.

It carries what a monitor can render off an unlocked read: identity, state, the driver that holds it, and counts. The offers themselves are reached by index through TryGetOffer(int, int, out DeviceOfferInfo), bounded by OfferCount; ResourceCount and InterruptCount are counts only.
