Interface IAudioOutput

Namespace
Cosmos.Kernel.HAL.Devices.Audio
Assembly
Cosmos.Kernel.HAL.dll

What an audio output driver implements and hands to PublishAudio(IAudioOutput). The kernel pushes frames in with Write(ReadOnlySpan<byte>) and the device drains them on its own clock, the mirror of Transmit(ReadOnlySpan<byte>): the driver owns the ring, the caller never blocks on it, and a short write is how the device says it is full. The driver reports the room it frees through BufferCompleted(). Called by the ring in thread context.
