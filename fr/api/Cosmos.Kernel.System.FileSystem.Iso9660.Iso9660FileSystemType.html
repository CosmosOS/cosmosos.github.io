Class Iso9660FileSystemType

Espace de noms
Cosmos.Kernel.System.FileSystem.Iso9660
Assembly
Cosmos.Kernel.System.dll

ISO 9660 driver entry point, for CD-ROM images and the volumes they are written to. Pluggable into the VFS via RegisterFileSystem(string, IVfsFileSystemType); mirrors FatFileSystemType and Ext2FileSystemType construction. The volume is read-only: a mount made through it refuses every mutation, and formatting or destroying one is refused.
