The identity of a Ps2 node: the port of the 8042 it hangs off, and nothing else. Paths are ps2:kbd and ps2:aux. Constructed by the 8042 driver in Cosmos.Kernel.Drivers, so the constructor is public, as PlatformIdentity's is.
