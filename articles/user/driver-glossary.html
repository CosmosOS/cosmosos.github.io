Driver glossary

These notes give the background on the hardware, operating system and build concepts that Writing a driver and the contributor page on the Driver manifest build on, for a reader who has not written a driver before. Each page explains the concept on its own, then closes with how Cosmos applies it. The table groups them in four parts: how a driver reaches a device, how the device and the driver signal each other, the buses, then how a kernel comes to carry a driver and picks one for each device. Interrupt context also builds on two scheduling notes from the contributor documentation, preemption and the instruction boundary.
